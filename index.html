Research Interests

I am a Ph.D. student at SecAI Lab, Sungkyunkwan University, advised by Prof. Hyungjoon Koo. My research focuses on the following areas:

  • AI Security & Privacy — security and privacy of machine learning models, including membership inference attacks (MIA) and machine unlearning.
  • Binary Reverse Engineering — analyzing and understanding binary code, and applying AI to reverse engineering tasks.
